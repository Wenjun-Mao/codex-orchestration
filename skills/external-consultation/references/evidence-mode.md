# Evidence Mode

Choose the consultation mode from the evidence needed, not from a presumed
universal ranking of tools or providers.

## Pro And Deep Research

Choose by the main unresolved need, not merely by whether a repository or
the internet is mentioned. These are working defaults, not exclusive
capabilities or a ranking of intelligence.

- Prefer **Pro** when the main value is deeper independent reasoning over
  identifiable evidence: exact repository or artifact inspection, reframing,
  option development and comparison, simplification, experiment design,
  correctness, causal or failure-path analysis, trade-offs, or critique of
  the goal, diagnosis, and proposed solution. It need not be a code-only or
  adversarial assignment.
- Prefer **Deep Research** when the main value requires substantial
  external discovery, comparison, and synthesis: primary research,
  approaches we may have missed, current capabilities, or evidence that
  could change the problem framing. Investigate the goal and constraints,
  not merely support for the current proposal.
- Consider **both** when you can name the additional contribution of a
  second consultation beyond what one well-scoped consultation can
  reasonably deliver. This may be complementary evidence or a deliberately
  justified independent assessment, not agreement for its own sake.
- Prefer **neither** when a bounded local inspection, experiment, or direct
  source lookup sufficiently addresses the intended need. Do not treat
  a local factual check as a substitute for requested independent judgment.

Match the choice to the modes' current capabilities and source access.
State unverified access assumptions; do not infer access from the mode
label or the preparing agent's credentials.

## Repository Or Artifact Inspection

Prefer a mode that can inspect the exact supplied files and revisions when the
question depends on code-grounded engineering analysis, ownership tracing,
artifact comparison, or concrete falsification paths. Require the report to
identify what it actually inspected and to separate direct observations from
inference.

## External Research

Prefer a research-capable mode when the question depends on current primary
sources, ecosystem discovery, standards, market context, or broad synthesis.
Specify source quality, recency, and citation expectations according to the
cost of error.

## Critique Or Possibility Generation

Use an independent reasoning assignment when the main value is reframing,
counterexamples, trade-off discovery, analogies, or alternatives. Give enough
evidence to avoid fantasy, while leaving room to challenge the current frame.

## Mixed Evidence

Mixed evidence does not automatically require two consultations. Use one
when it can adequately inspect and distinguish the relevant evidence.
Recommend both when the additional contribution justifies the research,
handoff, and integration effort.

Choose the arrangement from the dependency:
- **Parallel:** Each assignment can be usefully scoped and answered without
  the other's findings. Preserve independence when that is part of the purpose.
- **Deep Research then Pro:** External findings are needed to identify or
  refine the options before project-grounded evaluation.
- **Pro then Deep Research:** Project diagnosis is needed to identify the
  external questions worth researching.

If the second consultation's value depends on the first result, recommend
the first now and state what finding would justify the second. Do not
label a dependent second prompt ready until its required inputs exist.

Keep repository observations and external findings distinguishable. Do not
let broad research substitute for exact project evidence, or local source
inspection substitute for current external facts.

Other providers or product modes may fill the same evidence roles. Preserve the
role distinction even when the available labels differ.
