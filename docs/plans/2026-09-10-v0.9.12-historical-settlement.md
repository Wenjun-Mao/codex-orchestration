## Consequential decisions

1. **One read-side classification, no new stored lifecycle.** Authenticate each
   supported predecessor before choosing an executable source. Distinguish
   settled history, work requiring the existing source-owned path, and blocked
   or unsupported evidence. Aggregate every run across candidate namespaces;
   selection rank and checkout presence must not determine settlement. Multiple
   authenticated settled namespaces are admissible within existing bounds, without
   relaxing restrictions on multiple unfinished or unsupported predecessors.
2. **Facts stay with their owners.** Compose existing execution, audit/workflow,
   assignment/reporting, iteration/reclamation, and preservation validations.
   A closed run is not a retired assignment. A missing path or locator is not
   proof of authorized retirement. Do not duplicate closeout algorithms inside
   refresh or invent assignments for legitimately standalone executions. Reuse
   pure owner validations, not convenience calls that reconcile or repair history.
   Share facts, not a universal fully-retired requirement: sibling execution safety
   during an open assignment's refresh has different eligibility from fresh
   admission past entirely retired assignment history.
3. **Fresh permission is not deletion permission.** Fully settled predecessors
   remain unchanged and permit ordinary fresh activation without a refresh ID.
   Explicit preparation against fully settled history gives a non-mutating
   direction to fresh activation. Pending handoffs, live semantic refresh, and
   namespace deletion keep their distinct authority and lifetime requirements.
4. **Historical provenance is not live infrastructure.** Never substitute primary
   into a recorded runtime root, fabricate a baseline, or improve an old exporter
   retroactively. Keep live-source loading strict for actual unfinished work.
5. **Preserve the exact result in its authorized destination.** For the modern
   assignment-backed contract, reuse coordinator closeout's authenticated-primary
   ancestry relation; primary may advance while retaining the captured commit.
   Arbitrary reachability in any ref is insufficient. Name supported record
   shapes, exact execution bindings, and effective resource identities.
   Preserve genuine older-format contracts, including existing v0.8/v0.9.7
   support, through bounded readers; do not infer compatibility from a version
   prefix or silently rewrite a legacy preservation rule. Support the retained
   RC4 records, frozen v0.9.11 records, and the candidate's own emitted records
   consumed by stable explicitly.
6. **Revalidate at admission.** Inspection is advisory. Actual activation reruns
   the shared proof under the existing repository lock before target preparation.
   Keep the foreign-active-run sentinel and current final Git/ownership checks.
   Retained history remains subject to existing inventory/size bounds; expose
   applicable admission limits before partial target preparation.
