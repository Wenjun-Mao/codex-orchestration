# v0.9.7 exact archived-worktree reclamation

Status: Approved by the user on 2026-09-07.

This narrowly amends the approved assignment-reporting and lean-closeout plan.
Keep the original plan snapshot and historical release authorities unchanged.

## Outcome and decision

Complete accepted iteration cleanup without relying on an unverified promise
that Codex App will eventually remove an archived task's worktree. Flow may
reclaim an exact owned worktree after accepted work and authenticated archival.
Supersede ADR 0036's host-only reclamation rule only at this boundary; retain
the distinction between archival, physical reclamation, and branch cleanup.

## Scope and safeguards

- Apply to eligible iteration executors and disposable coordinators, including
  truthfully registered detached coordinators.
- Before removal, verify exact task/iteration ownership, archived/no-active
  evidence, Git common directory, canonical path, unchanged attachment and
  captured commit, clean state, and preservation in the authenticated
  integration baseline. Protect caller, primary, retained/shared, active,
  ambiguous, dirty, untracked-risk, and unintegrated work.
- Persist the exact cleanup intent through existing closeout authority and
  revalidate at mutation boundaries. Use non-force Git worktree removal;
  a refusal remains pending or blocked, not authority to force deletion.
- Remove only an eligible unchanged local task branch after worktree removal.
  Detached work grants no branch-deletion authority. Never touch remote refs,
  unrelated worktrees, or external side effects.
- Make interruption and already-absent reconciliation safe without repeating
  accepted archival. Retire reporting only when required closeout is complete.

## Bounded delivery and acceptance

The existing release coordinator owns implementation, focused regressions,
ADR/guidance alignment, packaging, and release. The director owns acceptance.
Do not rewrite journals or installed snapshots, perform ad hoc manual deletion,
change global App auto-delete settings, or add a daemon or cleanup registry.

Exercise exact named and detached cleanup, dirty/unpreserved/protected/path or
attachment drift rejection, host-already-removed state, interrupted removal,
and no archive replay. Reuse unaffected evidence; batch known corrections into
one candidate before the next necessary reload.

The cheapest safe direct proof is to resume the already accepted zero-child
canary through the corrected command, verify exact worktree removal and route
retirement, then complete the remaining real child-first canary. Do not repeat
the completed mutation. Stable promotion still requires the approved live and
release gates. Keep cleanup pending truthfully until those postconditions hold.

The temporary cleanup-observer timer is not a correctness dependency. Keep any
remaining watchdog limited to the user's original missed-report insurance,
with the existing restart/completion stop rules.

Escalate any need for force removal, weakened identity/preservation checks,
broader resource deletion, or materially wider cleanup authority.
