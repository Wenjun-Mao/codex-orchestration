# Stop policy

Stop and preserve evidence when continuing would require guessing authority,
replaying an ambiguous host action, broadening ownership, or hiding a
contradiction.

Before expanding instrumentation, name its primary outcome, causal question,
and cheapest safe direct attempt. After one supporting instrument-only
checkpoint, attempt that outcome or pause/replan. Further supporting
instrumentation requires explicit authorization.

An exact selector rejection before any task/agent identity permits one
selector-only child revision with a fresh operation. Ambiguity, transport
failure, any created identity, or post-creation mismatch does not.
