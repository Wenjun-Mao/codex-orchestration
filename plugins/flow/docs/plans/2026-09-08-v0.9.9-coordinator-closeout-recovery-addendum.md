# v0.9.9 coordinator closeout recovery delivery note

Status: Director-authorized delivery addendum, 2026-09-08.

## Outcome and scope

Complete all three approved recovery boundaries without recreating an old
worktree, adding a background lifecycle, or broadening predecessor support:
prevent a new wrong binding, correct an already-recorded one with authenticated
evidence, and recover accepted work when both coordinator resources are gone.

The correction is limited to:

1. host-backed coordinator worktree ownership before a route persists its
   cleanup binding;
2. an append-only binding correction from the exact assigned coordinator
   worktree after the director accepted its exact report;
3. a director-authorized absent-resource reconciliation that retains the exact
   accepted report and repeats either the pre-archive captured result tip or a
   uniquely assignment-bound completed coordinator-work final revision before
   normal native archival;
4. one v0.9.7-only, read-only settled-predecessor recognizer; and
5. clear missing-checkout source-authentication errors.

The live resource-loss reconciliation requires the effective registered
worktree and branch to be absent, the exact accepting director and report, and
the exact result tip already captured while the coordinator worktree existed
or recorded as its completed local-work final revision with passing verification.
It does not create an archive result,
branch ref, or integration claim; retrying `assignment accept` emits the normal
host archive action. If neither source exists, it fails with an actionable
manual director-disposition request. The historical v0.9.7 coordinator record
meets the completed-local-work contract (same run/runtime/configuration,
repository, plan revision, coordinator task, lost checkout, final revision,
and PASS verification), so the original pre-capture-loss shape is supported;
the rule does not treat its primary head, report text, or tag as result proof.
The separate refresh recognizer requires the exact retired assignment and plan, accepted report,
closed archived iteration, absent worktree and disposable branch, and a
non-host ref at the captured tip. Absence, an archived task, or a tag alone is
not sufficient. Any missing, stale, or contradictory proof blocks admission.

## Approved delivery exception

The installed v0.9.8 Flow operator cannot activate the exact closed v0.9.7
predecessor because its own coordinator checkout was lawfully reclaimed. This
implementation therefore runs on the dedicated v0.9.9 branch without an active
Flow run. It does not claim Flow activation, create receipts, alter predecessor
state, or use self-worktree/branch cleanup. Progress and final evidence are
reported manually to the director. Normal active-run Flow authority remains
required after this correction makes a future coordinator activatable.

## Regression and release guardrails

The focused regression separately exercises wrong-binding correction and
resource-loss recovery. It preserves the wrong initial primary binding while
closing only the authenticated worktree; it rejects absent resources with no
matching completed result, rejects an unrelated ancestor, persists the exact
completed coordinator-work authority, emits the normal archive request after
both resources disappear, completes closeout, and registers the next
same-release run. The predecessor lane still removes
the accepted report and preserved ref independently, verifies host-owner
mismatch rejection, and checks the actionable missing-checkout error. No new
framework, daemon, pilot mutation, or general predecessor reconciliation is
authorized.
