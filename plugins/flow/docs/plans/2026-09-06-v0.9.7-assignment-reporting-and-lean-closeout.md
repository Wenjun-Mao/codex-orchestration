## Outcome and cheapest direct attempt

A delegated coordinator can finish a bounded assignment, close its execution
run, perform authorized cleanup, and have its complete final reach the director
without reminders or monitoring. Reporting also survives an intervening
restart request and authenticated runtime refresh. A coordinator doing the
work itself can close honestly, without inventing an executor or abandoning a
successful run. Handoffs contain the minimum context sufficient for delivery.
Iteration membership is recorded by commands, and ordinary acceptance triggers
eligible closeout without a separate cleanup reminder. Role/iteration titles
make the retained tasks easy to recognize.

The causal question is whether reporting incorrectly shares the lifetime of
an execution run, while completion accounting incorrectly assumes every task
must launch a child. The cheapest safe direct attempt is one zero-child
assignment that proves its work, closes its run, then emits a real final which
the director receives. First reproduce that order with existing fixtures;
after the bounded correction, exercise it through the actual hook and queue.
Do not build a new diagnostics system or benchmark before this attempt.

## 1. Reporting belongs to the assignment

- Give the existing coordinator reporting authority an assignment lifetime,
  bound to exact approved-plan bytes, repository, sender, recipient, and
  recipient generation. Run/runtime bindings remain authenticated execution
  evidence, not the sole reason that upstream communication is valid.
- Run close, abandonment, and authenticated refresh must not prematurely
  terminate that coordinator-to-director route. Restart/blocker finals and
  cleanup finals remain reportable while the assignment is open. A final
  ending one turn is not necessarily completion of the assignment.
- Reuse the existing route, locator, report records, and locking primitives.
  Keep only the bounded reporting authority and immutable reporter material
  needed outside removable execution-run state. Do not retain whole old run
  namespaces merely to keep a communication channel alive. Report authority
  must not grant source mutation or revive a terminal run.
- Refresh authenticates any necessary reporter/runtime rebinding as part of
  the supported transition. Preserve assignment and sender-recipient binding
  while fencing old execution callbacks to their original run. Do not silently
  adopt whichever plugin happens to be installed, overwrite a conflicting
  locator, or leave a stale source pointer. Known authority conflicts stop.
- After the director reviews the actual final, the acceptance command records
  that decision and initiates eligible iteration closeout. Retire the route
  only when final reporting and required closeout are resolved, or through
  explicit safe cancellation. An accepted result with pending cleanup is not a
  closed iteration. Add no separate user approval ceremony or sender turn just
  to confirm its own archival.
- Persist exact report/acceptance and retirement evidence before removing the
  locator. Pending, ambiguous, conflicting, or manual-required reports continue
  to block ordinary retirement. Serialize capture, retirement, and rebind;
  recover crashes without replaying an uncertain queue attempt. A subsequent
  assignment in the same task receives fresh assignment authority. Late old
  events cannot be attributed to the new assignment.
- The hook still captures the complete final automatically at genuine task
  idle, including a blocked or restart-needed final. It never captures a
  `wait_threads` result, commentary, or an intermediate tool boundary. Deduplicate
  each source turn; do not suppress later legitimate finals of an open assignment.
  Do not require another summary, a second report file, or a model-authored send.
- Preserve same-local-host scope, exact untrusted envelopes, one-shot native
  queue submission, ordinary busy deferral, and the separate rare urgent path.
  Transport acceptance, recipient delivery, work verification, and director
  acceptance remain distinct. Do not claim end-to-end exactly-once delivery
  under an ambiguous host response.
- Integrate the bounded reporting state with exact refresh and unplug cleanup.
  No permanent route, orphan locator, broad task-history scan, new scheduler,
  watcher, daemon, or cross-release migration registry. Keep executor routes
  launch-bound; their disposition must not remove a still-required final report.

## 2. Represent coordinator-local work truthfully

- Extend existing workflow admission, claim, and verification accounting to
  represent explicitly coordinator-owned work. Bind it to the real coordinator,
  exact task/revision, run authority, declared ownership/resources, and Git
  baseline. Preserve overlap, dependency, and goal-proximate progress checks.
- Record completion with actual resulting revision or verified no-change proof
  and the required verification evidence. Native launch, child identity,
  executor callback, and executor integration fields are inapplicable, not
  fabricated. Do not create a dummy child, self-launch, or second executor
  lifecycle merely to satisfy the audit.
- A current closure audit must account for every task, including local work,
  before normal `run close`. Missing work, failed verification, stale revisions,
  pending children, or unresolved resources still block closure. Do not waive
  `workflow-task-unclaimed` generally or treat final prose as proof.
- Apply the same local-work contract when a workflow also contains children;
  zero-child success is not a caller-specific exception. Preserve existing
  child disposition/integration and exact Git cleanup responsibilities.
- Abandonment remains for genuinely abandoned work, not the documented success
  path for a completed coordinator-only assignment. Report delivery does not
  substitute for this closure proof or for director acceptance.

## 3. Command-managed iterations and acceptance-triggered closeout

An iteration is the user-facing group for one director assignment, not another
run or approval engine. Reuse assignment identity as its grouping authority;
give it a human-readable label. Maintain bounded repository-local JSON records
shared through the Git common directory, not one global fleet file.

- Commands register membership during authenticated dispatch/activation and
  reconciliation: exact host/task identity, role, reporting parent, iteration,
  and retained/disposable status. Reference existing launch, runtime, and Git
  evidence instead of duplicating it. Provisional membership never authorizes
  archive or deletion. Closing a reporting route does not erase membership.
- Models supply intent through typed commands, never hand-edit the registry.
  Use existing validation, locks, content addressing, and atomic persistence.
  Reject identity conflicts and illegal transitions; recover partial writes
  without inventing missing evidence or replaying ambiguous host operations.
- The org-chart belongs to assignment ownership. Reporting and cleanup consume
  it; neither discovers members from titles or assumes all tasks in a project
  belong to this iteration. Register only scoped Flow assignments; do not scan
  the fleet or retroactively adopt existing tasks.
- Generate requested titles from role, iteration label, and short purpose, for
  example `Executor · v0.9.7 · Reporting`. Keep role first, preserve a bounded
  readable length, and record observed titles without using them as identity.
  Retained director titles need not acquire a disposable iteration label.
  Do not rename existing user tasks as a release side effect.

Make cleanup a consequence of meaningful acceptance/finalization commands,
not another checklist the model or user must remember. Coordinator finalization
handles eligible accepted executors before normal run closure. After receiving
the complete final, director acceptance handles remaining eligible members and
the disposable coordinator. Both use the same scoped closeout logic; the
director performs judgment, not per-task bookkeeping.

The deterministic closeout path must:

- Resolve exact membership, final-result acceptance, live task activity, and
  Git ownership. Preserve the director, retained/shared tasks, unrelated tasks,
  and tasks whose identity or completion is unresolved.
- Archive eligible children before their coordinator. Authenticate tasks the
  user already archived as satisfied postconditions instead of duplicating
  host calls. Separate task archival from worktree reclamation and local-branch
  cleanup; only exact authorized, clean, integrated resources are eligible.
  Protect the caller checkout, source branches, remote refs, and unmerged work.
  Membership alone grants no discard authority.
- Execute and reconcile the actual native archive operations, not merely print
  intents and leave the model to reconstruct a loop. The current prepare/
  reconcile CLI is not evidence that this batch execution path already exists.
  Verify the bounded native adapter path before promising unattended archival;
  if it needs materially broader infrastructure, return that scope decision.
- Persist progress across per-task host operations. They are not one atomic
  transaction: failures, host-delayed reclamation, or ambiguous responses leave
  an honest resumable pending result. Resume on a later explicit invocation or
  an already-supported lifecycle trigger, not a new polling service. Do not
  promise every exceptional cleanup finishes without another trigger.
- Remove active locators and eligible iteration state only after required
  reporting and cleanup checks pass; retain the minimal acceptance/retirement
  evidence needed for idempotence and exact unplug. Cancellation retains its
  existing explicit authority and is not inferred from task idleness.

One acceptance operation is the normal interface; a scoped closeout/status
operation may serve recovery. No dashboard, web service, daemon, global
registry, automatic whole-project archive, or extra user reminder step.

## 4. Hybrid briefs: generated mechanics, authored meaning

Extend the existing generated-assignment approach to coordinator briefs rather
than introducing a general template engine. Commands assemble mechanical
fields from authenticated records: exact IDs, approved-plan digest/snapshot,
runtime references, reporting destination, title, and necessary entry commands.
The model supplies the task-specific outcome, scope, acceptance criteria, and
constraints, including reasons that change execution or escalation.

Use existing assignment structures, extending only necessary typed fields.
Do not ask the model to transcribe derivable authority or regenerate boilerplate.
Follow-ups contain the delta; plans/ADRs retain detailed deliberation. Preserve
explicit selector/rationale evidence without repeating a staffing essay to the
selected task. Generate only relevant instructions: a tool-generated long
message still costs the recipient input tokens.

Final results remain one complete model-authored report captured unchanged by
the hook. No second summary, rigid prose form, separate report-writing step,
hard token cap, or new telemetry. Add one concise before/after brief example.

## Acceptance and release gates

Automated tests must cover:

- Zero-child local mutation and no-change through current closure audit to
  normal close, plus mixed local/child accounting. Reject unclaimed work,
  overlap, missing/failed evidence, revision drift, and fabricated native IDs.
- Run close and abandonment followed by exact final capture/submission; a
  restart-needed final and authenticated refresh retain assignment reporting
  without retaining source journals or reviving old execution authority.
- Assignment acceptance/retirement ordering, concurrent capture/rebind/close,
  crash boundaries, same-sender reuse, late old events, stale recipients,
  tampered plans/reporters, namespace removal, and unplug residue checks.
- No report from waits/tool results; multiple genuine idle finals remain
  distinct. Preserve real adapter idempotency-key mapping, busy deferral,
  duplicate suppression, and ambiguous-delivery fail-closed behavior.
- Iteration records come from authenticated registration, survive route/run
  transitions, and reject forged membership, stale IDs, and conflicting writes.
  Generated titles remain display-only; retained and unrelated tasks survive.
- Acceptance-triggered closeout covers child-first archival, a disposable
  coordinator, manual prior archival, pending host reclamation, crash recovery,
  busy/shared tasks, dirty/unmerged resources, and repeat invocation without
  repeated host effects. Test actual caller-to-adapter contracts, not just a
  permissive mock. Pending closeout must not appear completed.
- Generated briefs preserve exact authority and action-changing reasons without
  redundant summaries. Review the shorter skills against approved behavior;
  no director-model directive, fixed staffing, or director monitoring loop.

Use one combined live same-host assignment where practical: a bounded
Terra-high coordinator performs a real small change with no children, verifies
it, closes the run normally, and completes coordinator-owned cleanup before
emitting its final. The director must actually receive matching complete bytes and
sender/assignment/turn identity. A restart-needed final must also arrive before
the user is expected to restart; after authenticated reload/refresh, the same
assignment continues reporting. Exercise ordinary busy deferral with a genuine
bounded director activity, not a waiting loop. The director then records
acceptance once; the command performs eligible coordinator archival, remaining
closeout, and exact reporting-route retirement without another sender turn.
Prove no active locator or release-owned cleanup residue remains and that late
old events stay fenced. Use real release-owned child tasks for the batch-archive
segment when available; otherwise add the smallest isolated child-bearing
canary needed. A zero-child test alone does not certify multi-member closeout.
Share live sessions and unaffected evidence instead of repeating the whole
release campaign for each addition.

This is the release-blocking sequence, not an active-route-only queue test.
Do not create synthetic executor authority, inject a report manually, or count
queue acceptance/manual retrieval as live delivery. If a required App-facing
segment fails, stable promotion stops until the corrected segment passes.
