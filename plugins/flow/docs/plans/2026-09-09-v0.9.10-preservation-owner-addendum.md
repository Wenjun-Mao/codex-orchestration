## Outcome and root cause

Allow accepted executor results to finish their real cleanup lifecycle when
the authenticated source/integration target is a coordinator delivery branch
newer than primary. No-change cleanup ignores the launch source; integrated
cleanup loses the named target in archive authority. Both then apply the
coordinator's final primary-preservation requirement prematurely.

Start from preserved RC2 source `1d5595621f1b6d5afaf3daa3f38cb359fa3f8759` on
the existing delivery branch. The cheapest direct proof is the actual cleanup
chain with old primary and a newer named delivery branch, not another broad
audit, harness or App restart.

## One preservation decision, existing evidence

- No-change executor: authenticate its accepted unchanged receipt, launch,
  disposition and verification. Require captured/final/starting revision
  agreement and preservation by the exact persisted launch source branch.
- Integrated executor: carry the existing reconciled integration's named
  target through archive authority. Require matching executor/result identity,
  completed verification, and preservation of the reconciled target tip by
  that exact target ref. Keep existing ancestor/patch-equivalent evidence;
  do not recompute patch similarity or substitute arbitrary surviving refs.
- Coordinator: retain the existing final primary-preservation requirement.
  Primary/source/caller protections remain separate from executor result-owner
  selection; do not weaken them to make a canary pass.

Resolve exact local refs, reject the disposable executor branch as its own
preservation target, and permit forward movement that retains the recorded
tip. Missing refs or non-descendant tips fail closed. Revalidate preservation
at the deletion boundary under existing locking and drift safeguards. Keep
child-first order, exclusive clean worktree checks, exact branch tips,
archived/no-active evidence and non-force removal. Archive success is not
permission to replay the host call.
